## Area: safest-product

### safest-product-1. Unknown-product safest mode reports 'out of age' when 2-dose candidates are actually complete (wrong per-candidate max-age verdict)

- **Classification (after verification):** ENGINE_BUG / high — CONFIRMED (reviewer: ENGINE_BUG / high)
- **Spec:** F05.3 (differing max-age conclusions -> ⚪ CHECK_PRODUCT_NAME), F05.4a, P1
- **Repro:** Default ruleset. Birth 2026-01-01; rota doses with no product on 2026-02-15 and 2026-03-20; today 2026-09-15 (37w). Actual: block=outOfAge, OUT_OF_AGE, nextDose #3. Expected: under Rotarix or Rotavin-M1 the child has completed (2 of 2 doses; with both doses = rotarix the engine itself returns bucket=complete). Under RotaTeq the child is out of age. The conclusions differ, so the result should be ⚪ CHECK_PRODUCT_NAME, not a firm 'too old'. Cause: in evalConcrete's max-age loop, nextFor(s.Plans, 'single', k=3, ...) fails for 2-dose series (NO_RULE_FOR_DOSE), so feasible=today and the 2-dose candidate is marked 'exceeded' when it should be 'complete'. At today 2026-07-01 (26w) the same bug gives exceededIn=rota-rotarix,rota-rotavin, which names the wrong products. Reproduced in probe/probe_test.go TestA.
- **Verification:** Reproduced (vsp/v_test.go Test1). Birth 2026-01-01, rota doses with no product on 2026-02-15 and 2026-03-20. Today 2026-09-15: block=outOfAge, OUT_OF_AGE. The same doses recorded as rotarix give bucket=complete; as rotateq they give outOfAge. F05.3 says that when possible products give different max-age conclusions, the result is ⚪ 'Kiểm tra tên vaccine' ('Check the vaccine name'), not 'too old', and F05.4a/P1 allow 'too old' only when certain. Today 2026-07-01: CHECK_PRODUCT_NAME exceededIn=rota-rotarix,rota-rotavin, which names the wrong products (those two are complete). Cause confirmed in engine.go max-age loop: nextFor(s.Plans,'single',k=3) fails for a 2-dose series, so feasible=today and it is past 24w/6m. Patched copy that skips series with n>=maxDoses: 2026-09-15 becomes ⚪ CHECK_PRODUCT_NAME exceededIn=rota-rotateq, and 2026-07-01 becomes overdue dose 3.
- **Proposal:** In the per-candidate verdict, treat a candidate with n >= maxDoses(candidate) as 'complete', never 'exceeded'. If any candidate is complete and another is out of age, return ⚪ CHECK_PRODUCT_NAME. Return outOfAge only when every candidate still needs a dose and every one is past its max age.

### safest-product-2. selectSeries drops products without a product-specific series when another dose's product has one (merged result less conservative than a candidate)

- **Classification (after verification):** ENGINE_BUG / high — CONFIRMED (reviewer: ENGINE_BUG / high)
- **Spec:** F05.3 'Phác đồ an toàn nhất' ('Safest series') (different products -> max over possible products)
- **Repro:** (1) Generic + specific: add a 2-dose series 'pertussis-infanrix' (product infanrix) and keep the generic 3-dose 'pertussis-primary' (used by hexaxim). Birth 2026-01-01; pertussis doses hexaxim 2026-03-01 and infanrix 2026-04-01; today 2026-04-20. Actual: bucket=complete, seriesUsed=[pertussis-infanrix]. Expected: safest over hexaxim (generic, 3 doses) and infanrix (2 doses) gives dose 3 (when product 2 is unknown, the engine does return upcoming dose 3). (2) Catalog product without a series: add catalog product 'rotasiil' (rota) with no series. Doses rotarix 2026-02-15 and rotasiil 2026-03-20; today 2026-04-10. Actual: complete, seriesUsed=[rota-rotarix]. If rotasiil is replaced by an unknown product, the engine gives upcoming dose 3. A known product therefore gives a less safe answer than an unknown one. Cause: the `if len(specific)>0 { pool = specific } else if len(generic)>0 {...}` logic drops any product whose doses map only to the generic series or to no series. Reproduced (TestC, TestD).
- **Verification:** Reproduced (Test2). (a) Added a 2-dose pertussis-infanrix series. Hexaxim 2026-03-01 + infanrix 2026-04-01, today 2026-04-20: complete, seriesUsed=[pertussis-infanrix]. The same doses with hexaxim+unknown give upcoming dose 3 due 2026-07-01, and hexaxim x2 gives dose 3 due 2026-05-01. (b) Added catalog product rotasiil with no series. Rotarix 2026-02-15 + rotasiil 2026-03-20, today 2026-04-10: complete, seriesUsed=[rota-rotarix]. Rotarix+unknown and rotasiil x2 both give upcoming dose 3. F05.3 says differing products use the max dose count over possible products. Code path: `if len(specific)>0 { pool = specific }` discards the generic or no-series fallback of other records. Shows a series as complete while a dose is still needed.
- **Proposal:** Resolve the candidate series per dose record: its specific series, else the generic series, else treat it as an unknown product (the whole disease pool). Then take the union across records. Do not let one record's specific series hide another record's generic or unknown fallback.

### safest-product-3. Soft-deleted / out-of-catalog product loses its own (stricter) series: patient shown complete

- **Classification (after verification):** MODEL_LIMIT / medium — RECLASSIFIED (reviewer: MODEL_LIMIT / high)
- **Spec:** F11.3 (soft-deleted product counts as unknown product, F05.3), F04.1 out-of-catalog product
- **Repro:** Birth 2026-01-01; rota doses product=rotateq on 2026-02-15 and 2026-03-20; today 2026-05-01. With RotaTeq in the catalog: upcoming dose 3 (due 2026-07-01). After RotaTeq is soft-deleted, the snapshot cannot contain the series of a product that is not in the catalog (New() errors), so rotateq and rota-rotateq are removed. Actual: bucket=complete, seriesUsed=[rota-rotarix, rota-rotavin], SAFEST_SERIES over the remaining products only. The dose's real product needs 3 doses. Product has no 'deleted' flag either. - **Verification:** Reproduced (Test3). Rotateq doses 2026-02-15 and 2026-03-20, today 2026-05-01. With rotateq in the catalog: upcoming dose 3 due 2026-07-01. With the rotateq product and series removed from the snapshot: complete, SAFEST_SERIES products=rotarix,rotavin, and no PRODUCT_NOT_IN_CATALOG reason (FreeProduct is empty). Emit PRODUCT_NOT_IN_CATALOG for a retired product ID as well. For free-text products, decide in the spec whether 'possible products' can exceed the catalog. If it can, the result should be ⚪ rather than complete.

### safest-product-4. No doses yet: differing maxAgeStart across products gives ⚪ CHECK_PRODUCT_NAME instead of a reminder listing the products still in age

- **Classification (after verification):** ENGINE_BUG / medium — CONFIRMED (reviewer: ENGINE_BUG / medium)
- **Spec:** F05.3 'Chưa tiêm mũi nào → gợi ý theo bệnh, liệt kê các sản phẩm phù hợp với tuổi' ('No doses yet → recommend by disease, list the products suitable for the age')
- **Repro:** Ruleset change (illustrative value only): rota-rotateq maxAgeStart=12w. Birth 2026-01-01; no rota doses; today 2026-04-05 (13w4d). Actual: bucket=unknown, CHECK_PRODUCT_NAME exceededIn=rota-rotateq, products=[rotarix, rotateq, rotavin], nextDose #1 due 2026-03-01. Expected: 🔴 dose 1 with products filtered to rotarix and rotavin, which are still startable until 15w (2026-04-16). With no doses there is no product name to check, and the child is about 11 days from losing the window while the card shows only ⚪. Cause: with n == 0 the whole disease pool goes to safest mode, and the max-age disagreement rule built for unknown recorded products is applied to future product choice. Reproduced (TestF).
- **Verification:** Reproduced (Test4) with rota-rotateq maxAgeStart=12w, birth 2026-01-01, no doses, today 2026-04-05. Result: bucket=unknown, CHECK_PRODUCT_NAME exceededIn=rota-rotateq, products=[rotarix,rotateq,rotavin]. With the default data (all 15w), the same input gives overdue dose 1. ## Area: dates-validity

### dates-validity-1. A level-4 (unknown time) dose lets a dated dose that is definitely too early count, so the series shows as complete

- **Classification (after verification):** ENGINE_BUG / high — CONFIRMED (reviewer: ENGINE_BUG / high)
- **Spec:** F05.10 (level-1 dose violating min age -> not counted, ⚪); F04.2 (level-4 doses excluded from ordering)
- **Repro:** Measles, birth 2026-01-01 (day), today 2026-11-10. Records: U = measles, precision unknown (level 4), no number; A = measles, day 2026-07-01 (age 6 months, before the 9-month minimum), no number. Actual: bucket=complete, reason COMPLETE. Control with only A: unknown/DOSE_INVALID. Expected: ⚪ DOSE_INVALID. If A is dose 1 it is before the minimum age. If A is dose 2, dose 1 (U) came even earlier. Either way a dose is invalid. Cause: orderDoses puts undated doses first, so A gets position j=2 in validate. The dose-2 rule has no minAge, and its minInterval is skipped because prev was reset to nil by the undated dose. Then n=2>=count gives complete before hasUndatedUnnumbered is ever checked. Reproduced in <scratchpad> (case A).
- **Verification:** Reproduced in my own copy (verify-dates-validity-skeptic/cmd/skeptic). Measles, birth 2026-01-01, today 2026-11-10. U is level 4 with no number; A is on 2026-07-01 (day) with no number. Actual: complete/COMPLETE. Control with A alone: unknown/DOSE_INVALID dose 1. orderDoses sorts undated doses first, so validate checks A as j=2. The dose-2 rule has no minAge, and prev was reset to nil, so nothing is checked. Every possible order breaks F05.10. If A is dose 1, it is before minAge 9m. If U came before A, U was given before 6 months of age, so no valid dose 1 can exist before A. 'Complete' therefore contradicts F05.10 and P1.
- **Proposal:** When any level-4 dose has no number, the position of each dated dose is unknown. Check each dated dose against every position it could hold (at least the dose-1 minAge, which is the earliest bound). Alternatively, treat 'level-4 present and the dated dose fails any possible position' as DOSE_INVALID or POSSIBLY_INVALID before the COMPLETE check.

### dates-validity-2. A numbered level-4 dose is assumed to come before unnumbered dated doses, so the next dose is computed from a dose that may not be the latest

- **Classification (after verification):** ENGINE_BUG / high — CONFIRMED (reviewer: ENGINE_BUG / high)
- **Spec:** F04.2 level 4 (next-dose due cannot be computed -> ⚪); F04.1 numbering
- **Repro:** Pertussis, birth 2026-06-01, today 2026-09-20. Records: U = pertussis, precision unknown, DoseNumber=2; A = pertussis, day 2026-08-01, no number. A is therefore dose 1 or dose 3, and U (dose 2) may be later than A. Actual: bucket=due, nextDose 3 with earliest 2026-08-29 and due 2026-10-01 (computed from A). Expected: ⚪ PREVIOUS_DOSE_DATE_UNKNOWN. If U was given on 2026-09-15, dose 3 is not safe before 2026-10-13, so the engine marks it 'due' earlier than safe. Same pattern with A on 2026-10-20 and today 2026-11-10: upcoming, earliest 2026-11-17. Cause: hasUndatedUnnumbered ignores numbered undated doses, and the evalConcrete prev = ordered[last] only because the sort puts undated doses first.
- **Verification:** Reproduced. Pertussis, birth 2026-06-01, today 2026-09-20. U is level 4 with DoseNumber=2; A is on 2026-08-01 with no number. Actual: due, next dose 3 earliest 2026-08-29 (computed from A). Same pattern with A on 2026-10-20 and today 2026-11-10: upcoming, earliest 2026-11-17. Control with A numbered 1: unknown/PREVIOUS_DOSE_DATE_UNKNOWN, which is correct. In the unnumbered case, F04.1 chronology makes A dose 1, so its next dose follows the level-4 dose 2. F04.2 level 4 says the next due date cannot be computed (⚪). If U was given on 2026-09-15, dose 3 is not safe before 2026-10-13, so 'due' on 2026-09-20 is earlier than safe. Cause: hasUndatedUnnumbered ignores numbered undated doses, and validate also gives A position j=2, the same position as U.
- **Proposal:** In mixed numbering, place numbered doses at their numbers and unnumbered dated doses in chronological order around them. If a level-4 dose may be the latest dose (its number is greater than or equal to the possible position of the last dated dose), return PREVIOUS_DOSE_DATE_UNKNOWN. Or simply treat any undated dose whose position relative to the dated doses is not fixed as blocking.

### dates-validity-3. Duplicate or out-of-order user dose numbers skip the minimum-interval check and give 'complete'

- **Classification (after verification):** ENGINE_BUG / high — CONFIRMED (reviewer: ENGINE_BUG / high)
- **Spec:** F04.4 (user number contradicts chronology -> save with warning, consequence per F05.10); F05.10 (min-interval violation -> not counted, ⚪)
- **Repro:** Rota, birth 2026-07-01, today 2026-10-01, two Rotarix doses on 2026-08-20 and 2026-08-25 (5 days apart, minimum 4 weeks). (a) Both DoseNumber=1: actual complete/COMPLETE. (b) Earlier dose unnumbered, later dose DoseNumber=1: actual complete/COMPLETE. Control with no numbers: unknown/DOSE_INVALID (dose 2). Expected: ⚪. The doses are 5 days apart, and the numbering contradicts the record count or chronology. Cause: validate sets j=d.number (or i+1 for unnumbered), so both records are checked against the dose-1 rule (minAge only) and the interval is never checked. n=len(doses)=2 then reaches count.
- **Verification:** Reproduced. Rota (Rotarix), birth 2026-07-01, today 2026-10-01, doses on 2026-08-20 and 2026-08-25. (a) Both numbered 1: complete/COMPLETE. (b) First dose unnumbered, second numbered 1: complete. (c) No numbers: unknown/DOSE_INVALID dose 2. F04.4 says a user number that contradicts chronology is saved with a warning, and the consequence follows F05.10 (a level-1 min-interval violation is not counted, ⚪). The engine raises no warning reason, never checks the 5-day gap, and counts n=len=2 to reach 'complete'.
- **Proposal:** Reject or flag duplicate positions: if two records resolve to the same dose number, or user numbers are not increasing in chronological order for non-overlapping dates, return ⚪ with a NUMBERING_CONFLICT reason. Always check minInterval between chronologically adjacent dated doses whatever the numbering.

### dates-validity-4. A dose that follows an implied or level-4 dose is never interval-checked, so a possibly invalid dose counts toward 'complete'

- **Classification (after verification):** SPEC_GAP / medium — CONFIRMED (reviewer: SPEC_GAP / medium)
- **Spec:** F05.10 (covers only levels 1-3); F05.4 ⚪ ('mũi có thể không hợp lệ' ('dose may be invalid')); F04.1 implied level-4 doses
- **Repro:** HPV (Gardasil 9), birth 2000-01-01, today 2026-11-10. (a) Dose #1 on 2026-01-05 and dose #3 on 2026-06-10, with dose 2 implied: actual complete (IMPLIED_EARLIER_DOSES, COMPLETE). (b) The same with an explicit level-4 #2 record: complete. (c) No numbers: dated 2026-01-05, a level-4 record, dated 2026-06-10: complete. Dose 2 could have been on 2026-05-01, which puts dose 3 only 6 weeks after it (minimum 12 weeks). In (a) the engine checks dose 3's minInterval against dose 1 instead. In (b) and (c) prev is reset to nil, so nothing is checked. Expected: undefined in the spec. By P1 and the F05.4 ⚪ definition, this arguably should be ⚪ (possibly invalid).
- **Verification:** Reproduced. HPV (Gardasil 9), birth 2000-01-01, today 2026-11-10. #1 on 2026-01-05 and #3 on 2026-06-10, with #2 implied: complete with IMPLIED_EARLIER_DOSES. With an explicit level-4 #2: complete. All unnumbered with a level-4 dose in the middle: complete. The spec does not cover this. F05.10 covers only level 1-3 doses, and F04.2 level 4 says 'tính là đã có mũi' ('counts as a dose given'). The ⚪ entry 'mũi trước ở mức 4' ('previous dose at level 4') in F05.4 is about the next dose, not about the validity of a recorded dose, so SPEC_GAP is correct. One part is a plain logic slip: in case (a) validate checks dose 3's 'minInterval from previous dose' against dose 1, a non-adjacent dose. That is less strict than the true rule, not conservative.
- **Proposal:** Decide in the spec whether a dated dose right after a level-4 or implied dose is 'possibly invalid' (⚪) or trusted. Then in validate, either treat minInterval as unverifiable (POSSIBLY_INVALID) or explicitly accept it with a reason code. Never check the dose-j interval against dose j-2.

### dates-validity-5. With implied earlier doses, the first recorded dose is used as 'dose 1' and the result is not marked estimated

- **Classification (after verification):** ENGINE_BUG / medium — CONFIRMED (reviewer: ENGINE_BUG / medium)
- **Spec:** F04.1 (user number > records -> earlier doses are level 4); F04.2 (level 4 -> next due cannot be computed; levels 2-5 -> 'ước lượng' ('estimated') label)

The engine computes the firstDose+6m anchor and the minIntervalFromFirst=5m bound from the dose-2 date, because evalConcrete sets first=ordered[0]. Expected: dose 1 is level 4 (implied), so anchors measured from the first dose cannot be computed (⚪ PREVIOUS_DOSE_DATE_UNKNOWN, or at least a reason code), and estimated=true. - **Verification:** Reproduced. HPV, birth 2000-01-01, today 2026-11-10, one Gardasil 9 record on 2026-06-01 (day) with #2. Actual: due, next dose 3 earliest 2026-11-01, due/overdue 2026-12-01, estimated absent (false), reasons only IMPLIED_EARLIER_DOSES. evalConcrete uses the #2 record as 'first', so the firstDose+6m and the 5m bounds are anchored on the wrong dose. ### history-and-model-8. Catch-up ('tiêm bù nếu chưa tiêm' ('catch-up if not yet vaccinated')) series: a nil StartAge acts as a wildcard, so with an exact birth date an adult gets the infant series (due 1991) and the false reason AGE_AT_START_UNCERTAIN, and forgotChildhood hides the adult catch-up

- **Classification (after verification):** ENGINE_BUG / low — RECLASSIFIED (reviewer: MODEL_LIMIT / medium)
- **Spec:** F05.2 'Tiêm bù theo tuổi' ('Catch-up by age'); F05.5 (more-doses tiebreak only when the age range straddles a boundary); F04.5 (prioritize what can be done right away at the current age, MMR…); F05.10 tolerance
- **Repro:** Added measles-adult (stage adult, startAge {18y,120y}, 2 doses) next to the existing measles-primary, which has no startAge. Birth 1990-04-01 (exact), HistoryStatus none, no doses, today 2026-11-10. Actual: seriesUsed [measles-primary], nextDose 1 due 1991-01-01, reason AGE_AT_START_UNCERTAIN{candidates: measles-primary,measles-adult, chosen: measles-primary}. Birth is exact, so nothing is uncertain, and the adult catch-up series should be chosen. With forgotChildhood, the same tie picks the childhood series, allChildhood() is true, and the result is FORGOT_CHILDHOOD. The adult MMR catch-up that F04.5 says to prioritise is suppressed. Also: DoseRule has no Tolerance field (F05.10). Remaining-dose count cannot depend on age at the current dose (Hib/PCV-style catch-up), because brackets apply only to age at first dose.
- **Verification:** Repro matches. measles-adult {18y–120y} plus an unbracketed measles-primary, birth 1990-04-01 exact. Under none: measles-primary, due 1991-01-01, AGE_AT_START_UNCERTAIN. Under forgotChildhood: FORGOT_CHILDHOOD. But the model CAN express this. In a control run measles-primary got startAge {0d,18y}, and measles-adult was selected under both none and forgotChildhood (overdue, due 2008-04-01). So this is not a MODEL_LIMIT. The real defect is in selectSeries. It applies the F05.5 'more doses' tiebreak and emits AGE_AT_START_UNCERTAIN whenever two or more series of one product group match, even with exact dates. F05.5 limits that to a range straddling a boundary. Equal dose counts fall back to slice order, and New() does not reject overlapping or missing brackets. The outcome is never earlier than safe, hence low. Missing Tolerance equals the spec default of 0. The spec does not explicitly require age-at-current-dose dose counts.
- **Proposal:** Add Series.Kind "routine"|"catchUp". New() rejects two series of the same disease and product whose StartAge brackets overlap or are missing. Emit AGE_AT_START_UNCERTAIN only when possiblyWithin is true for more than one bracket because the birth or dose range is wide. Under forgotChildhood, evaluate catchUp series normally instead of short-circuiting. Add DoseRule.Tolerance *Span (+citation) for validate(). Add DoseRule.SkipIfAgeAtDoseAtLeast *Span (or MaxAgeAtDose) for age-dependent catch-up dose counts.
