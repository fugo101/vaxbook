## VNVC (vnvc.vn) as a data source

### 1. Price list URL and how the page is built
- **Main price list:** https://vnvc.vn/bang-gia-tiem-chung/. Its title is "BẢNG GIÁ TIÊM CHỦNG VẮC XIN TẠI VNVC THÁNG 11/2026" and it lists about 64 vaccines and biologics. The note on the page says prices apply "từ ngày 30/10/2026 cho đến khi có thông báo mới".
- **Rendering:** The site runs on WordPress. robots.txt disallows `/wp-admin/` and the sitemap says "generated by Rank Math SEO Plugin" (https://vnvc.vn/sitemap_index.xml). WebFetch pulled the full table text, which suggests the table is server-rendered HTML rather than built by JS. **UNVERIFIED:** I could not inspect the raw source.
- **Cloudflare:** A plain `curl` to the price list got **HTTP 403 "Attention Required! | Cloudflare"**. I did not try to get around the block. Any automated fetcher should expect bot protection.
- **JSON API:** The WordPress REST endpoints `https://vnvc.vn/wp-json/wp/v2/pages?slug=bang-gia-tiem-chung` and `.../posts?slug=...` both returned `[]`. The page may be a custom post type, or REST may be restricted. **No public JSON price API was found.**
- **Change tracking:** The sitemaps carry `lastmod` dates (e.g. https://vnvc.vn/page-sitemap.xml) that could be used to spot content changes.

### 2. Fields
**Price table columns** (https://vnvc.vn/bang-gia-tiem-chung/):
- STT (row number)
- Phòng bệnh (disease)
- Tên vắc xin/Sinh phẩm (product name)
- Nước sản xuất (country of manufacture)
- Giá ưu đãi tiêm lẻ, VNĐ/liều (single-dose price)
- Giá đặt trước theo yêu cầu, VNĐ/liều (pre-order price, about 1.2x)
- Tình trạng (stock status, e.g. "Đầy đủ" or "Đầy đủ*")

Example row: "Zona thần kinh | Shingrix | Bỉ | 3.680.000 | 4.416.000 | Đầy đủ".

**Data quality:** The page has internal inconsistencies. A banner says Envacgen is made in "Thụy Sĩ" while the table says "Đài Loan". Validation rules are needed before relying on these values.

**Package prices ("gói"):** Packages are on separate pages as static tables.
- Each package shows: package name, age group, injection timepoints, and per vaccine the disease, brand, country and doses. It also shows "Giá gói", the discount, "Giá gói sau ưu đãi" and "TỔNG THANH TOÁN".
- The stated formula is "Giá Gói vắc xin = Tổng giá trị các mũi tiêm lẻ trong gói + Phí dịch vụ gói".
- Example: the pregnancy package costs 8.230.800, or 7.901.568 after discount.
- Package pages:
  - https://vnvc.vn/goi-vac-xin-danh-cho-tre-em/
  - https://vnvc.vn/goi-vac-xin-cho-tre-tien-hoc-duong/
  - https://vnvc.vn/goi-vac-xin-cho-tuoi-vi-thanh-nien/
  - https://vnvc.vn/goi-vac-xin-danh-cho-nguoi-lon/
  - https://vnvc.vn/goi-vac-xin-danh-cho-phu-nu-chuan-bi-mang-thai/
  - https://vnvc.vn/goi-vac-xin-cho-phu-nu-mang-thai/

### 3. Regional or center differences
- **Prices:** The price list says it applies system-wide, and no regional prices were found (https://vnvc.vn/bang-gia-tiem-chung/).
- **Stock:** Stock status is "cập nhật theo từng thời điểm và từng trung tâm" (updated by time and by center). Items marked "*" need a call to the hotline or the center to confirm. **The app cannot reliably show per-center stock.**
- **Third-party claim:** One third-party article says Qdenga prices vary by time and center (https://tamanhhospital.vn/tiem-chung/gia-vac-xin-sot-xuat-huyet/). **UNVERIFIED** against VNVC.

### 4. robots.txt and terms of use
- **robots.txt** (https://vnvc.vn/robots.txt):
  ```
  User-agent: *
  Disallow: /wp-admin/
  Allow: /wp-admin/admin-ajax.php
  Sitemap: https://vnvc.vn/sitemap_index.xml
  ```

  It does not restrict price pages, but Cloudflare blocks non-browser clients in practice.
- **Copying notice:** Pages carry this notice: "Các thông tin trên website vnvc.vn chỉ phục vụ cho mục đích tham khảo, tra cứu… Mọi động thái sao chép chưa được sự chấp thuận chính thức của VNVC đều là trái phép". The footer reads "Bản quyền ©2016 thuộc về CÔNG TY CỔ PHẦN VACXIN VIỆT NAM" (https://vnvc.vn/lich-tiem-chung-cho-tuoi-vi-thanh-nien-va-nguoi-lon/).
- **No terms-of-use page found:** The policy sitemap lists only the payment policy and the privacy policy (https://vnvc.vn/chinhsach-sitemap.xml). Both are **UNVERIFIED** for anything about scraping.
- **Implication:** Copying prices automatically without permission carries legal risk. Options are to ask VNVC for written permission or a data partnership, or to have staff enter a small set of facts (price, product) by hand, with a link and date shown. The final call needs legal review.

### 5. Recommended schedules by age group
- **Children:** https://vnvc.vn/lich-tiem-chung-day-du-nhat-cho-tre-theo-tung-thang-tuoi/
  - Organised by month of age, from 0 to 18 months, then 2–3 years and 4–8 years.
  - Gives doses and intervals, and says late doses should be given as soon as possible.
  - Does **not** separate free government (TCMR/EPI) vaccines from paid ones.
  - Cites Decision 2470/QĐ-BYT (14/6/2019) for screening before vaccination only.
  - Last updated 30/07/2025.
- **Adolescents and adults:** https://vnvc.vn/lich-tiem-chung-cho-tuoi-vi-thanh-nien-va-nguoi-lon/
  - A table for ages 9–18 plus adult recommendations, with dose intervals (e.g. MMR 2 doses at least 1 month apart, Shingrix 2 doses 1–2 months apart).
  - No update date is shown.
- **General guide:** https://vnvc.vn/cam-nang-tiem-chung/lich-tiem-chung/ (not fetched).
- **Pregnancy:** There is no pregnancy schedule with timing. The two pregnancy package pages list only vaccines, with no gestational week or months-before-conception wait:
  - Pregnant: Boostrix, TT, Vaxigrip, Abrysvo.
  - Planning pregnancy: ProQuad, Qdenga, Prevenar 20, and others.
  - Timing must come from Bộ Y tế, WHO or other official sources.

### Takeaways for scoping
- **Use VNVC for:** a price snapshot and the product-to-disease-to-country mapping. Store the "as of" date and link back to the price list.
- **Do not use VNVC for:** clinical rules. Its content is commercial, mixes free and paid vaccines, and has inconsistencies. Base the recommendation engine on the Ministry of Health (Bộ Y tế) schedule, TCMR and WHO.
- **Risks:** Cloudflare blocks automated fetching, and copying without approval is prohibited. Legal or partnership review is recommended before ingesting any data automatically.
- **Out of scope for this step:** The user's new points (web push for reminders, and users who don't know exact dose dates or even the year) don't depend on VNVC data.

## Long Chau (Tiêm chủng FPT Long Châu) as a data source

Checked 2026-11-10 using curl and WebFetch. Anything I could not confirm is marked UNVERIFIED.

### 1. Catalog and price URLs, and how the data is delivered
- **Main catalog:** https://tiemchunglongchau.com.vn/vacxin. Disease and category pages follow the same pattern, e.g. https://tiemchunglongchau.com.vn/vacxin/6in1 and https://tiemchunglongchau.com.vn/vacxin/phong-cum. Product pages look like https://tiemchunglongchau.com.vn/vacxin/vac-xin-gardasil-9-05ml.
- **Packages:** https://tiemchunglongchau.com.vn/vacxin?type=combo
- **Price-list pages:** https://tiemchunglongchau.com.vn/pages/bang-gia-tiem-chung and https://tiemchunglongchau.com.vn/pages/bang-gia-tiem-chung-cho-be
- **Product list:** https://tiemchunglongchau.com.vn/sitemap_product.xml. It had 59 URLs today and points to a sitemap index at https://tiemchunglongchau.com.vn/sitemap.xml.
- **Rendering:** the site is built with Next.js (App Router). Page HTML contains data embedded as React Server Components (RSC, the `self.__next_f` payload), so a category page and its first products arrive with the page, server-rendered.
- **Product pages** also carry schema.org Product data in JSON-LD: `price` (e.g. Gardasil 9 = 2,965,000 VND), `priceCurrency`, `availability`, `manufacturer`.
- **Price table on /pages/bang-gia-tiem-chung:** the per-vaccine table (searchable by disease, filterable by country of manufacture) is **not** in the server HTML. It is loaded by JavaScript. Only the package prices are server-rendered.
- **JSON API (found in the site's JavaScript, not called by me):**
  - The base is `https://api.tiemchunglongchau.com.vn/gw/v1/public/...`, including `vac-web-bff-before-order`.
  - Endpoints: `POST /product/vaccines` (product list), `GET /product/combos`, `GET /product/combo-by-id`, `GET /common/product-suggestion` (the "Tôi nên tiêm gì" tool), `/shop/suggest-shop-vaccine`, `/location/regulated-province`.
  - It is UNVERIFIED which base each endpoint uses, what the request bodies are, and whether they need authentication.

### 2. Fields available (from the embedded page data)
- **Product:** `name`, `title`, `sku`, `slug`, `brandName`, `manufacturers[]`, `national` and `nations[]` (country of manufacture), `unit` (e.g. "Lọ", a vial), `image`
- **Disease:** `diseaseGroup`, `diseaseGroupId`, `categoryDisplayName`, `categoriesData[]` (a product can belong to several disease groups, which fits our many-to-many design)
- **Price:** `currentPrice`, `originalPrice`, `discount`, `labelPromotions[]` (with promotion codes and FPT staff-only prices). Example: Nimenrix 1,700,000, was 1,750,000. Infanrix Hexa and Hexaxim: 995,000.
- **Schedule:** `regimens[]`, each with an age-range `title` and `requiredInjection`. Example, Gardasil 9: "Từ 9 - dưới 15 tuổi" → 2 doses; "Từ 15 - dưới 46 tuổi" → 3 doses.
- **Stock:** `isPreOrder`, `status` (empty in the samples), JSON-LD `availability: InStock`. Stock at each center is UNVERIFIED; `/shop/suggest-shop-vaccine` may cover it.
- **Packages:** name, `totalAmount`, `finalAmount`, `totalDiscountAmount`. Example: "GÓI COMBO 3 THÁNG", 12,422,400đ, down from 12,985,500đ.

### 3. Regional price differences
- **UNVERIFIED.** Each product has a single national `currentPrice`, and I found no province selector that affects price. The province and store endpoints appear to be for finding centers and booking.
- Long Châu blog articles call prices "reference" and say they change over time and with promotions. I found no source saying prices differ by province ([example](https://nhathuoclongchau.com.vn/bai-viet/gia-vac-xin-bach-hau-ho-ga-uon-van-bao-nhieu-tien-mot-mui.html)).
- Promotions are tied to specific groups (e.g. the FPT staff offer), so the app should show `currentPrice` and note that it is a reference price.

### 4. robots.txt and terms of use
- **https://tiemchunglongchau.com.vn/robots.txt** (fetched verbatim) applies to all crawlers and blocks `/tim-kiem`, `/*?search`, `/*?_rsc=`, `/tra-hang/`, `/huy-coc/`, `/dang-xu-ly/`, `/ttdh/`, `/chi-tiet-lich-tiem/`. Product and category pages are allowed. Fetching the RSC data endpoint (`?_rsc=`) is disallowed.
- **https://nhathuoclongchau.com.vn/robots.txt** (summarized) blocks search, cart, orders, order lookup and lab results. Plain curl requests to `/tiem-chung` and `/vac-xin` on that domain returned **403**, which suggests bot protection.
- **Terms of use:** [Quy chế hoạt động](https://tiemchunglongchau.com.vn/chinh-sach/quy-che-hoat-dong-website-ung-dung-thuong-mai-dien-tu-ban-hang) says all content belongs to Trung tâm Tiêm chủng FPT Long Châu, and "Nghiêm cấm mọi hành vi sao chép, sử dụng và phổ biến bất hợp pháp…" (unlawful copying, use or distribution is prohibited). It also reserves the right to change prices. The site shows a DMCA badge.
- I found no clause about scraping or APIs, and no open-data licence. **Legal review is recommended** before storing their prices. The safer approach: store only facts (product name, price, date checked), link to the source page, and don't copy descriptions or images.

### 5. Age-based schedules
- **Yes, published in three forms:**
  - **The "Tôi nên tiêm gì" tool** (https://tiemchunglongchau.com.vn/toi-nen-tiem-gi) uses 7 age groups with `ageMin`, `ageMax` and `ageUnit`: 0–<6 months (in weeks, 0–26), 6–<12 months, 12–<24 months, 2–<9 years, 9–<45, 45–<65, and 65+.

  - **Age-based packages:** "7 gói theo độ tuổi" (7 packages by age).

## Authoritative sources for Vietnam's vaccine schedule (as of 2026-11-10)

### 1. National EPI (TCMR) schedule and its legal basis
- **Thông tư 10/2024/TT-BYT** was issued 13/06/2024 and took effect 01/08/2024. It replaced TT 38/2017 and lists **11 diseases**, with recipients and schedule in its appendix. Key schedule points: HepB monovalent within 24h of birth; BCG within 1 month; DTP-HepB-Hib at 2, 3 and 4 months (doses ≥1 month apart); DTP booster at 18 months; reduced-dose (Td) booster at 7 years; OPV from 2 months; IPV at 5 and 9 months; measles at 9 months; MR at 18 months; JE 3 doses; Rota oral from 2 months. Tetanus for pregnant women is covered, but the dose details are UNVERIFIED. [cantho.gov.vn summary](https://pbgdpl.cantho.gov.vn/danh-muc-benh-truyen-nhiem-doi-tuong-bat-buoc-phai-su-dung-vac-xin), [thuvienphapluat](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/The-thao-Y-te/Thong-tu-10-2024-TT-BYT-benh-truyen-nhiem-pham-vi-phai-su-dung-sinh-pham-y-te-bat-buoc-613510.aspx)
- **Thông tư 13/2026/TT-BYT** took effect **01/07/2026**. Its list has **14 items**: the 11 above plus **pneumococcal disease** and **HPV**, and "other diseases set by the Minister". [baochinhphu](https://en.baochinhphu.vn/hpv-vaccine-added-to-mandatory-immunization-list-111260520165031478.htm), [lsvn](https://lsvn.vn/dua-hpv-vao-danh-muc-tiem-chung-bat-buoc-tu-ngay-01-7-2026-a173076.html), [vneconomy](https://vneconomy.vn/quy-dinh-14-benh-phai-su-dung-vaccine-sinh-pham-trong-tiem-chung-bat-buoc.htm)
  - One report gives the issue date as 16/05/2026: UNVERIFIED.
  - It reportedly replaces TT 24/2018, 34/2018, 05/2020 and 52/2025. Whether it supersedes TT 10/2024: UNVERIFIED.
  - The PCV and HPV dose schedule in the appendix: UNVERIFIED. I could not get the official text.
- **QĐ 2780/QĐ-BYT** (29/08/2025) is the EPI plan for 2026–2028. It lists 14 vaccines (OPV and IPV counted separately), including PCV and HPV, and was later amended by QĐ 3245. [luatvietnam](https://luatvietnam.vn/y-te/quyet-dinh-2780-qd-byt-cua-bo-y-te-ban-hanh-ke-hoach-tiem-chung-mo-rong-giai-doan-03-nam-2026-2028-410147-d1.html)
- **Rollout differs by region.** Rota started in 32 provinces in late 2024 and is aiming for nationwide coverage in 2026; doses must be finished before 6 months of age. PCV and HPV start in priority (mountainous or ethnic-minority) areas. [tuoitre](https://tuoitre.vn/mien-phi-loai-vac-xin-dat-do-them-vu-khi-phong-benh-cho-tre-nho-20260123095138251.htm). HPV for girls aged 11 is from news reports only: UNVERIFIED. [tuoitre](https://tuoitre.vn/se-tiem-vac-xin-ngua-hpv-mien-phi-cho-tre-em-gai-11-tuoi-tu-2026-20251221185656116.htm)
- **Modeling implication:** "Free under TCMR" depends on the date and the province, not only on the disease. Store an effective-from date and a scope note for each entry.

### 2. Free (TCMR) vs paid (service)
- **TCMR (free):** the 13 named diseases above, using specific products (for example the domestic Rotavin-M1 for rota: UNVERIFIED in EPI documents).
- **Service (paid):** everything else, including MMR, varicella, HepA, meningococcal, influenza, dengue, typhoid, rabies (outside outbreaks), RSV, zoster, and the 6-in-1 and PCV products bought privately. No official "service list" exists. Build it from the price catalogs.

### 3. Schedules for paid (service) vaccines
- There is **no Bộ Y tế or Hội Y học Dự phòng lifecycle schedule** that I could find. VNAPM only co-authored a policy report. [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/vn/vi/insights/2024/10/life-course-immunization-in-vietnam.html)
- **VNVC** publishes a child schedule (0 months to 8 years, about 25 entries) with no update date and a "reference only" note. [vnvc.vn](https://vnvc.vn/lich-tiem-chung-cho-tre/). I could not find a VNVC adult page.
- **Long Châu** has a vaccine catalog, a "tôi nên tiêm gì" tool (this is a direct competitor feature) and price pages. [tiemchunglongchau](https://tiemchunglongchau.com.vn/), [/toi-nen-tiem-gi](https://tiemchunglongchau.com.vn/toi-nen-tiem-gi), [/pages/bang-gia-tiem-chung](https://tiemchunglongchau.com.vn/pages/bang-gia-tiem-chung)
- **Recommendation:** use the product labels (the Vietnamese package insert) as the source of truth for paid vaccines. Use clinic pages only as secondary sources.

### 4. International fallbacks
- **WHO Tables 1–4** (Table 1 updated December 2025) are PDFs only, so rules must be coded by hand. They are not meant for direct use by health workers. [WHO](https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/table-1-who-recommendations-for-routine-immunization), [Table 3, interrupted/delayed](https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/table-3-recommendations-for-interrupted-or-delayed-routine-immunization-summary-of-who-position-papers)
- **CDC CDSi** is the most machine-readable option: Logic Specification plus Supporting Data, with test cases. [cdc.gov/iis/cdsi](https://cdc.gov/iis/cdsi/index.html). **Caution:** the US schedule changed in 2025–2026 outside the usual ACIP process, and a court stayed it on 16/03/2026. It is legally unstable. [CRS](https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/IN12684.html)
- **Recommendation:** use WHO as the primary fallback and CDSi only as a design reference for the rules engine.

### Tricky facts for the data model
- **Rota:** Rotarix is 2 doses, finished by 24 weeks. RotaTeq is 3 doses, finished by 32 weeks. Rotavin-M1 is 2 doses, finished before 6 months. [drugs.com](https://www.drugs.com/dosage/rotavirus-vaccine.html), [AU handbook](https://immunisationhandbook.health.gov.au/contents/vaccine-preventable-diseases/rotavirus). WHO says to finish with the same product and not to start after 24 months. Switching products is allowed only if unavoidable (WHO Table 1).
- **HPV (Gardasil 9):** ages 9–14 get 2 doses, 6–12 months apart, with a 3rd dose if the gap was under 5 months. Ages 15–45 get 3 doses at 0, 2 and 6 months. [gardasil9.com](https://www.gardasil9.com/adults/?p=35), [Tâm Anh](https://tamanhhospital.vn/tiem-chung/lich-tiem-hpv/). WHO allows a single dose off-label for ages 9–20. The Vietnamese 9–45 approval details: UNVERIFIED.
- **The dose count depends on age at the first dose** (HPV, PCV catch-up, and Hib not needed after age 5). The engine therefore needs `product × age-at-start → series`.
- **Minimum intervals and maximum ages** differ by product. Store both: `min_age`, `max_age_start`, `max_age_complete`, `min_interval`.
- **Interrupted series:** resume without restarting (WHO Table 3).
- **Unknown dates (user forgot even the year):** CDC accepts self-reported doses without a written date only for influenza and pneumococcal polysaccharide vaccine. [immunize.org](https://www.immunize.org/ask-experts/topic/documenting-vaccination/). Suggestion: add a `date_precision` field (day/month/year/unknown), give a lower-confidence recommendation, and tell the user to confirm with a clinician.

I could not open the official TT 13/2026 text, so the 2026 PCV/HPV schedules need checking by a qualified person.

### Differentiation points
1. **Works across chains:** A neutral record that holds doses from the public program, VNVC, Long Châu, abroad and paper books together. Each chain only shows its own doses.
2. **Recommendations that cite their source:** Adults, catch-up, pregnancy and planning pregnancy. These run on rules from the cited schedule, not on what a chain sells.
3. **Side-by-side prices per disease and per product:** This seems to be unfilled. The data needs normalizing: VNVC lists single-dose and pre-booking prices, and Long Châu's web prices include the 10% fee.
4. **Doses with unknown dates:** Allow a dose to be "given, date unknown", "year only", "approximate age" or "not sure". The engine then handles it conservatively and tells the user to confirm with a clinic. None of the apps reviewed showed this feature.
5. **Lightweight and private:** A PWA usable without logging in, with email and web-push reminders, a consent screen and delete-all-data. This contrasts with the chains' login friction.

## Legal & compliance (VN)

## Vietnam legal/compliance constraints for the vaccine-tracking MVP

This is not legal advice. Most of the sources below are secondary (law-firm alerts, translations, Q&A sites). Anything marked **UNVERIFIED** I could not confirm in a primary text.

### 1. Personal data protection
- **Which rules apply now.** The PDP Law No. 91/2025/QH15 took effect **2026-01-01** (Art. 38(1)). Decree **356/2025/ND-CP** implements it and **replaced Decree 13/2023** from the same date. Consents collected under Decree 13 stay valid (Art. 39). [LuatVietnam EN – Law 91](https://english.luatvietnam.vn/dan-su/law-on-personal-data-protection-law-no-91-2025-qh15-405135-d1.html), [Vietnam Briefing – Decree 356](https://www.china-briefing.com/china-outbound-news/vietnam-personal-data-protection-regulation-decree-356)
- **Health data is sensitive.** Decree 356 Art. 4(1)(d) lists "health status" as sensitive data. Vaccination records most likely fall under this, but no text names vaccination explicitly (**UNVERIFIED**). Law Art. 26(1)(a) requires consent to process health information. [LuatVietnam EN – Decree 356](https://english.luatvietnam.vn/decree-no-356-2025-nd-cp-dated-december-31-2025-of-the-government-detailing-a-number-of-articles-and-measures-for-the-implementation-of-the-law-on-p-422896-doc1.html)
- **Consent.** Consent must be voluntary, informed, explicit and given separately for each purpose. Silence is not consent (Law Art. 9). Decree 356 Art. 6 bans default or pre-ticked consent, requires the consent to be verifiable, and requires telling users when the data is sensitive. The consent screen needs separate toggles for: health data, reminders, and (if ever) marketing.
- **Family and child profiles.** Legal representatives exercise data rights for children (Law Art. 24). For children aged 7 and over, both the child and the guardian must consent, but only for disclosing private information. How this applies to an adult managing a parent's or another adult's profile is **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Data subject rights and deadlines** (Decree 356 Art. 5):
  - Acknowledge every request within 2 working days.
  - Access or correction: 10 days.
  - Withdrawal of consent or objection: 15 days.
  - Deletion: 20 days.
  - The planned delete-all-data feature fits these.
- **Impact assessment dossier.** File within 60 days of starting processing (Law Art. 21). Small and startup businesses may opt out for 5 years, and micro-enterprises and household businesses are exempt. **Neither relief applies if you directly process sensitive data** (Art. 38(2)–(3)). This MVP does, so expect to file. One source says "large number of data subjects" means more than 100,000 people. I could not find that number in the decree text (**UNVERIFIED**).
- **Cross-border transfer.** Storing data on a foreign cloud provider counts as a cross-border transfer (Decree 356 Art. 17(1)(a)). A transfer impact dossier is due within 60 days of the first transfer (Law Art. 20). Cloud data must be encrypted at rest and in transit (Decree 356 Art. 12(4)). Google login, foreign email or push providers, and analytics tools probably also count (**UNVERIFIED**).
- **Breaches and penalties.** Report breaches within 72 hours. Fines go up to VND 3 billion, or 5% of the previous year's revenue for cross-border violations. Individuals are fined half the amount for organizations (Law Art. 8).
- **Individual developers.** An individual is still a data controller. No specific exemption for individuals was found (**UNVERIFIED**).

### 2. Health-information site licensing and vaccine advertising
- **Website licence.** Under Decree 147/2024 (effective 2024-12-25), only "aggregated information" sites (*tổng hợp*) and social networks need a licence. Internal, personal and specialised-service sites (including health) are exempt (Art. 24). If the catalog republishes third-party content, it could be classed as an aggregated site (**UNVERIFIED**). [LuatVietnam](https://luatvietnam.vn/linh-vuc-khac/co-nhung-loai-trang-thong-tin-dien-tu-nao-loai-trang-nao-khong-can-cap-phep-883-100184-article.html)
- **Vaccines are drugs** under the Law on Pharmacy, and the Advertising Law treats vaccine ads as drug ads. [luatvietnam – consolidated Pharmacy Law](https://luatvietnam.vn/thuc-pham/van-ban-hop-nhat-76-vbhn-vpqh-2026-hop-nhat-luat-duoc-430282-d5.html)
  - Older rules required the Ministry of Health to confirm ad content (Art. 79). The 2024 amendment changed the procedure, and the current regime for vaccines is **UNVERIFIED**. [thuvienphapluat](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/hoi-dap-phap-luat/co-so-nao-duoc-dung-ten-trong-ho-so-de-nghi-xac-nhan-noi-dung-quang-cao-thuoc-205060.html)
  - The amended Advertising Law, effective 2026-01-01, makes whoever disseminates an ad responsible for its accuracy. [lsvn](https://lsvn.vn/luat-sua-doi-bo-sung-mot-so-dieu-cua-luat-quang-cao-chinh-thuc-duoc-quoc-hoi-thong-qua-a159106.html)
- **Main risk.** Showing prices with links to VNVC or Long Chau, or "recommendations", may be treated as vaccine advertising (**UNVERIFIED**). Mitigations: show no ranking or promotion, keep the content neutral, cite official sources (Ministry of Health or the national immunization programme), and add a disclaimer.

### 3. Scraping and republishing prices
- I found no Vietnamese law or court ruling that deals specifically with scraping public, non-personal price data. Three areas carry risk, all **UNVERIFIED**:
  - Breaching the source sites' terms of use.
  - Copyright in compiled content (Vietnam reportedly has no separate database right). [UNGOVR tracker](https://law.ungovr.org/ai/vn)
  - Unfair competition rules (an amendment is under discussion). [MLex](https://www.mlex.com/mlex/articles/2540338)
- Scraping personal data is high-risk. [Tilleke 2023](https://www.tilleke.com/insights/vietnams-data-protection-regulations-what-to-expect-in-2023/3/)
- **Safest approach:** get written permission or a data feed from each chain, collect only prices, show a "last updated" date and a link to the source, honour robots.txt and keep request rates low.

### 4. Marketing vs. transactional notifications
- **Advertising under the PDP Law** (Art. 28): needs consent, an opt-out, an immediate stop on request, and consent for behavioural tracking. Decree 356 lists behavioural tracking data as sensitive. [Rouse](https://rouse.com/insights/news/2026/vietnam-key-developments-in-personal-data-protection-under-decree-no-356-2025-nd-cp-and-issues-businesses-need-to-review)
- **Anti-spam (Decree 91/2020):** advertising email requires prior consent and an opt-out, and is limited to 3 per address per 24 hours (Art. 13). [hethongphapluat](https://hethongphapluat.com/nghi-dinh-91-2020-nd-cp-ve-chong-tin-nhan-rac-thu-dien-tu-rac-cuoc-goi-rac/dieu-13)
  - Whether the decree exempts transactional email (vaccine reminders) is **UNVERIFIED**.
  - It covers SMS, email and calls. Whether it covers **web push** is **UNVERIFIED**.
  - A draft replacement decree from the Ministry of Public Security exists, and its status is **UNVERIFIED**. [bocongan.gov.vn](https://bocongan.gov.vn/media/bca-media/library-20260909084715-c3d4dec4-0f78-459d-96de-3293d10b69b5-4-ban-so-sanh-du-thao.docx)
- **Recommendation:** treat reminders as a service the user opts into, with separate consent from any marketing. Do not put promotional or price content in reminders.

### Questions for a qualified Vietnamese lawyer
1. Is vaccination history "health status" under Decree 356 Art. 4, and do the 5-year startup relief or micro-enterprise exemption still apply?
2. The impact assessment dossier and cross-border transfer dossier for the chosen hosting, email, push and OAuth providers. Whether to host data in Vietnam.
3. How consent works for family profiles, covering minors, other adults, and pregnancy flags.
4. Whether price display, product listings or recommendations count as vaccine advertising, and whether a website licence is needed under Decree 147.
5. Scraping vs. licensed data from VNVC and Long Chau, including their terms of use and the IP and unfair-competition risk.
6. Whether reminder emails and web push count as transactional, and the status of the Decree 91 replacement.
7. How the disclaimer limits liability for incorrect recommendations.

Note: the user has said "users may not remember dates (even the year)". Store approximate or unknown dates honestly, and make the recommendation logic conservative so the product does not appear to give medical advice. That is my suggestion, not a legal requirement.

## Completeness critique

## Feature-scope critique

### (a) Missing features and edge cases (most important first)
1. **Doses the user only half-remembers.** For each dose, let the user enter the date as an exact day, a month, a year, an approximate age, or "don't know". Let the brand be "don't know", with the dose then recorded against the disease. Add a status of "possibly given". The engine should treat these doses cautiously and show "Cần xác nhận với cơ sở y tế" (confirm with a clinic). CDC accepts undated self-reported doses only for influenza and PPSV ([immunize.org](https://www.immunize.org/ask-experts/topic/documenting-vaccination/)).
2. **Combination products and doses outside the catalog.** A 6-in-1 dose has to count toward every disease it covers. Doses given abroad, or copied from a paper book with a product we don't list, need a free-text entry that the user maps to diseases.
3. **Status per disease.** Show done, due now, upcoming, overdue, "too old to start" (for example rotavirus, [WHO Table 1](https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/table-1-who-recommendations-for-routine-immunization)), and "can't assess".
4. **Pregnancy flag that expires.** Ask for a due date or gestational week and clear the flag automatically afterwards. Planning-pregnancy needs a review date too. Suppressing live vaccines during pregnancy should follow the product label (UNVERIFIED which products).
5. **Version the rules.** Each recommendation shows its source and the date of the rule set. When rules change (for example [TT 13/2026](https://en.baochinhphu.vn/hpv-vaccine-added-to-mandatory-immunization-list-111260520165031478.htm)), recalculate.
6. **Free (TCMR) badge.** Whether a vaccine is free depends on the date and the province, so show a scope note with the badge.
7. **Web push has limits.** On iOS it works only after the user adds the app to the Home Screen, needs iOS 16.4 or later, and permission must come from a user tap ([WebKit](https://webkit.org/blog/13878/web-push-for-web-apps-on-ios-and-ipados/)). We need an install guide, email as the fallback, and one digest per family instead of one push per dose.
8. **Guest-to-account.** When a guest signs up, move their local data into the account. Add export or print of a summary to bring to a clinic.
9. **Delete a single profile,** not only all data. Record consent for each adult profile managed by someone else (UNVERIFIED legal basis).

### (b) Over-scoped for MVP: cut or reduce
- **Automated crawling of VNVC and Long Châu.** Both forbid unapproved copying ([VNVC](https://vnvc.vn/bang-gia-tiem-chung/), [Long Châu](https://tiemchunglongchau.com.vn/chinh-sach/quy-che-hoat-dong-website-ung-dung-thuong-mai-dien-tu-ban-hang)). Instead, enter about 20 key products by hand each month with an "as of" date, or show links only until we have permission.
- **Package prices, pre-order prices, promotions and stock.** Link out instead.
- **Schedules for every product.** Cover the TCMR diseases plus a short list of paid vaccines (HPV, PCV, influenza, rotavirus, MMR, varicella, HepA). Everything else shows "tham khảo bác sĩ" (ask a doctor).
- **Two login methods.** Email OTP alone is enough, since reminders need an email address anyway. Google login adds a cross-border data flow (UNVERIFIED).
- **Timing advice in pregnancy by gestational week.** Leave it out until an official Vietnamese source is verified.

### (c) Biggest risks
1. **Medical correctness (highest).**
   - There is no official lifecycle schedule for paid vaccines ([KPMG](https://kpmg.com/vn/vi/insights/2024/10/life-course-immunization-in-vietnam.html)).
   - The PCV/HPV dosing in the TT 13/2026 appendix is UNVERIFIED.
   - The number of doses depends on age at the first dose.
   - Mitigation: a clinician signs off every rule, there is a test suite of cases, wording stays neutral, and a disclaimer is shown.
2. **Legal and privacy.**
   - Vaccination history is probably sensitive health data under [Decree 356](https://english.luatvietnam.vn/decree-no-356-2025-nd-cp-dated-december-31-2025-of-the-government-detailing-a-number-of-articles-and-measures-for-the-implementation-of-the-law-on-p-422896-doc1.html).
   - Expect to file an impact assessment and a cross-border transfer dossier within 60 days.
   - Push services and a foreign cloud may count as cross-border transfers (UNVERIFIED).
   - Showing prices next to a recommendation could be treated as vaccine advertising (UNVERIFIED).
   - Get a lawyer's review before launch.
3. **Data freshness.**
   - VNVC's prices take effect month by month. Long Châu's web prices include a 10% fee ([tiemchunglongchau](https://tiemchunglongchau.com.vn/)), so the two are not directly comparable.
   - Show an "as of" date and a stale badge. Don't rank cheapest first.
4. **Retention.**
   - Doses are months or years apart, and guests have no reminders.
   - iOS push needs the app installed, so email is the reminder channel we can actually count on. Watch email deliverability.
   - Competitors: chain apps with booking, and [VNeID](https://baolaocai.vn/30-trieu-so-suc-khoe-tren-ung-dung-vneid-dam-bao-phai-thuc-chat-thiet-thuc-post896535.html) records that fill in automatically.
